# Domain glossary

Use these words, in code and in conversation. Ambiguity here turns into bugs.

## Marketplace

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Trip** | A journey a driver has published. Has a route, a departure time, seats, and a contribution. Never call it a "ride" in code. |
| **Booking** | One passenger's claim on one or more seats of a trip. |
| **Contribution** | What a passenger pays the driver for a seat. Deliberately not "fare" or "price" — those words imply a commercial transport service, which is the legal position we must not concede. |
| **Service fee** | What abro charges. Collected as the deposit. |
| **Deposit** | The portion paid online at booking, sized to cover the service fee. |
| **Balance** | The remainder of the contribution, paid in cash to the driver at pickup. |
| **Boost trip** | A synthetic trip generated by adding a short detour to a published one, so a passenger off the corridor can still match. |
| **Detour tolerance** | How much extra distance and time a driver will accept. Set per trip by the driver. |
| **Corridor** | An intercity route pair, e.g. Addis Ababa – Bahir Dar. |
| **Meeting point** | A gazetteer entry where driver and passenger actually meet. Never a free-text address. |
| **Approval mode** | Whether bookings are confirmed instantly or need the driver's assent. |
| **No-show** | Failure to appear within 15 minutes of the agreed meeting time. |

## Money

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Santim** | The minor unit of the Ethiopian birr. 1 ETB = 100 santim. **All amounts are integer santim.** Never a float, anywhere, ever. |
| **ETB** | Ethiopian birr, the only currency. |
| **Ledger entry** | A double-entry accounting row. Every movement of money produces two. |
| **Payout** | Money sent to a driver. Only relevant for online-contribution trips, which v1 does not have. |
| **PSP** | Payment service provider — Chapa, Telebirr, M-Pesa Ethiopia. |
| **NBE** | National Bank of Ethiopia. Its directives are why we cannot hold customer funds. |

## Places and geography

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gazetteer** | The curated table of known places. Our substitute for a geocoder, because Ethiopia has no street addresses. |
| **Menaharia** (መናኸሪያ) | Bus terminal. The most important kind of meeting point. |
| **Woreda / Kebele** | Ethiopian administrative districts and wards. Useful as labels; their boundaries shift, so do not rely on them as geometry. |
| **Gross match** | Stage one of matching. Cheap, indexed, as-the-crow-flies, in PostGIS. |
| **Exact match** | Stage two. Real detour computed by Valhalla, on survivors only. |
| **Route fraction** | Where a point falls along a trip's route, 0 to 1, via `ST_LineLocatePoint`. Pickup must come before dropoff or the trip is going the wrong way. |

## Identity and trust

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fayda** (ፋይዳ) | Ethiopia's national digital ID. Integrated over OIDC. Required for drivers. |
| **Code 1 / 2 / 3** | Ethiopian vehicle plate categories. Code 2 is private, and is what carpooling depends on — and the legally contested category. |
| **Blind reveal** | Ratings stay hidden until both sides rate or 14 days pass. Prevents revenge ratings. |
| **Verified** | Means only that a procedure was completed. It is never a claim that the information is true, and the UI must say so. |

## Time and language

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **EAT** | East Africa Time, UTC+03:00, no daylight saving. |
| **Ethiopian time** | The 6-hour dawn-offset convention. 09:00 EAT is spoken as "3:00 in the morning". Lives only in `@abro/time`. |
| **Ethiopian calendar** | Thirteen months, 7–8 years behind Gregorian. New year is Meskerem 1, around 11 September. |
| **Pagumē** (ጳጉሜ) | The thirteenth month. Five days, six in a leap year. |
| **Bahire Hasab** (ባሕረ ሃሳብ) | The computation for movable religious feasts. Those feasts drive intercity travel demand. |
| **Ge'ez numerals** | ፩ ፪ ፫. Acceptable for dates. Never for money or seat counts. |
| **`am_normalize`** | The Postgres function folding Amharic homophones so `ሰላም` and `ሠላም` match. Every gazetteer name query goes through it. |
